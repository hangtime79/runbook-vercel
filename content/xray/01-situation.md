## What
Slide 1 of the opening deck: three big numbers (confirmed fraud, the 46-merchant cluster, the fraud rate) above the closing line. The fraud count and rate are read from the data when the page loads.

## Tell
Tell the story in two sentences: a fraud wave, investigators built their own tools, one cracked a ring.

## Say
This app is one of those tools.

## Head of Fraud
After a fraud wave, investigators built their own apps. One cracked a major ring. They've fought for years for better tools and more development.

## CIO
Watching apps appear that IT either gets asked to build or inherits after the fact. Doesn't want to be cut out.

## Show
Start on the app's root URL; it opens `/intro` full-screen. Move with the arrow keys.

## Craft
Open with the customer's story, not a feature list. The app is introduced as one of the tools in that story, so the audience is already inside it when the demo starts.
