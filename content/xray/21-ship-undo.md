## What
The third stop on the Change control card: merging to production, and rolling back.

## Tell
Merge, and the change goes to production. Then show the undo. This beat needs a production deployment in place.

## Say
If it's wrong, rollback happens at the routing layer, within seconds, without a rebuild.

You're not cut out. You set the rails once. Every new app, from IT, from the fraud team, or from an agent, inherits them. You stop inheriting apps; you host them from day one.

## CIO
You are not cut out. You set the rails once and every new app inherits them.

## Leave the app

## Craft
Close the act on the audience's own fear. The last words answer "I keep inheriting apps I didn't build".

## Watch out
On Hobby you can roll back to the previous deployment. Rolling back to any deployment is a Pro feature.
