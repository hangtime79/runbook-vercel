## What
Slide 2: the Head of Fraud and the CIO each get one line, and the APRA quote sits under them as the worry they share: AI in development (agents and investigators writing code) and AI in usage (AI inside the tool).

## Tell
Look at each of them in turn: Head of Fraud, "keep the speed"; CIO, "I keep inheriting apps I didn't build." Read the APRA line aloud.

## Say
The volume and speed of AI assisted software development is placing strain on the effectiveness of change and release management controls.

## Head of Fraud
Wants to keep that speed: investigators keep building. Fears being shut down by IT or risk.

## CIO
Wants common infrastructure, easy development and oversight. Fears shadow IT, AI risk and the next APRA review.

## Craft
Name the shared worry before offering anything. Each person hears their own line first, then the one worry that joins them.

## Watch out
Re-check the APRA quote against the PDF before the demo. It came through a page summariser.
