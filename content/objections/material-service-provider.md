## They say
"If this goes to production, Vercel goes on our CPS 230 register. Do you have the paperwork for that?"

## Why they ask
Contract terms have to give APRA access to documentation and allow an on-site visit, and the bank has to notify APRA about material arrangements. They don't want to start that process for a pilot, or find out late that the terms don't fit.

## Answer
Whether it's a material arrangement depends on whether this tool supports a critical operation. A fraud-investigation app probably doesn't, but that call is yours, not mine. What I can tell you is what the platform gives you as evidence: SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001:2022 and PCI DSS through the Trust Center, a 99.99% SLA on Enterprise, and rollback in seconds. What I can't tell you is whether the contract grants APRA's access rights or covers offshoring. Those are Enterprise sales conversations, and I'd start them early.

## Don't say
Don't imply any APRA endorsement of Vercel. Don't say Vercel's DPA grants APRA access.
