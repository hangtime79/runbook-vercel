## They say
"We have a low-code platform and an internal container platform. Why add another?"

## Why they ask
Every new platform is more to patch, audit and fund. The CIO has spent years consolidating.

## Answer
If an existing platform already serves your investigators, use it; adding another isn't the goal. Power Platform gives IT a governance console, and its data policies are connector-level guardrails that are off until you set them. Retool prices per builder and can be self-hosted. The question is what your investigators actually build. This app is a Next.js app with a native analytics engine and an AI tool loop, written by coding agents from a runbook. The test is whether your current platform gives that same identity, review, logging and rollback to code that agents write. If it does, stay. If it doesn't, that is the gap.
