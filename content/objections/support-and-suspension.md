## They say
"I have heard of accounts being paused with no warning, and of slow support unless you pay a lot."

## Why they ask
An internal tool that investigators depend on cannot vanish on a billing question, and CPS 230 asks about service levels.

## Answer
Vercel's help page lists four causes of a pause: a spend limit your own team set, usage beyond plan quotas, a policy violation (Terms, Acceptable Use, Fair Use; Hobby is for non-commercial use only), or a platform incident. For policy pauses it emails you with details and next steps, and the fair-use page says that, where possible, it reaches out before acting on unreasonable usage. It also says that if no email arrives it can't discuss the pause. Projects do not resume on their own. Spend Management only pauses production if you switch that on, and it does not stop AI Gateway usage. On support, Vercel's terms offer Hobby, Standard, Premium and Enterprise levels, and commit to response targets only for Enterprise subscriptions; Enterprise also lists a dedicated Success Manager and an SLA. A production bank app belongs on the contract tier. I did not find a page saying pauses are automatic on traffic spikes or billing anomalies.

## Show
Nothing live. Point to the tier slide.

## Don't say
Don't quote response times in hours until you have read the support-terms table yourself. Don't say pauses are automatic on spikes.
