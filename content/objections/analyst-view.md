## They say
"Is Vercel a safe choice? What do Gartner and Forrester say?"

## Why they ask
A bank's third-party review leans on analyst standing, and a vendor's own claims carry less weight.

## Answer
Two Gartner placements, both reported on Vercel's own pages quoting Gartner. Vercel was named a Visionary in the 2024 Magic Quadrant for Cloud Application Platforms, published 4 November 2024, and a Visionary in the 2025 Magic Quadrant for Cloud-Native Application Platforms, published 4 August 2025. Visionary is not Leader, so I would present it as strong on direction and ask about maturity. Forrester's Wave on Edge Development Platforms, Q4 2023, evaluated twelve vendors including Vercel; Forrester's announcement gives no placements and I did not read the report, so I have no rating to quote. Gartner states it does not endorse any vendor in its research.

## Show
Nothing live.

## Don't say
Don't call Vercel a Leader. Don't quote a Forrester Wave placement or Gartner Peer Insights reviews. Don't present these as Gartner's own pages; they are Vercel's.
