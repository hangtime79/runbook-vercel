## They say
"APRA wants an AI inventory, model monitoring, and human oversight. What do I hand them?"

## Why they ask
The letter says few entities have operationalised AI governance. The CIO needs artefacts, not a promise.

## Answer
Three things you can hand over. An inventory: the gateway logs every call with model, provider, tokens, latency and cost, per project, so the list of what this app calls is generated, not maintained by hand. Oversight: the AI only reads and shows its evidence, and a human decides. Purpose control: every refusal is logged with the reason, so you can show the tool stayed in scope. Be clear about the limit: the inventory of AI use cases across the bank is your job. This covers one app.

## Show
The Ask button, then the evidence card and its per-answer model, time and cost line.

## Don't say
Don't say this satisfies APRA's expectations. Say it gives you evidence to show.
