## They say
"Every time IT adds control, we lose the speed that cracked the ring. I've been shut down before."

## Why they ask
The fear is that governance becomes a queue and the tool gets taken away.

## Answer
The rails are the reason you keep building. The CIO sets identity, regions, approved models and checks once; every app inherits them, so your team doesn't negotiate each time. A change gets a private preview of the real thing, so your team reviews it live. The checks are ones you define, and they run in the pull request. What you give up is shipping straight to production with no check. What you keep is an investigator turning an analysis into a tool, which is how this one was made. A bounded tool is one you're allowed to keep.

## Show
The thesis slide, then the deployment badge and the PR's checks.

## Don't say
Don't promise faster delivery than the demo shows.
