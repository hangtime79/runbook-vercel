## Notes on the two rows the review flagged

**V-56, the memory line.** The review said `bill-at-scale.md` contradicted R-02 and VP §6. Against the live page, the card was right and the other two were loose. The page says memory bills "continuously until the last in-flight request finishes" and then "no CPU or memory charges apply until the next invocation". So "memory bills while a request is in flight" and "nothing is charged between requests" are both correct. What the card left out is that memory keeps billing while requests wait on I/O, and that overlapping requests share one instance. VP §6 ("memory keeps billing for the life of the instance") overstated it. VP and the card are both reworded.

**V-33, the 5 GB limit.** Not stale. The limitations page still labels 5 GB "Large functions Beta", and the 2026-06-29 changelog also calls it public beta. VP was right to say beta. What was missing: the changelog date, that new projects are auto-enrolled, and that it does not work with Secure Compute or Static IPs.
